Welcome to Trinity

A Community Built on Faith, Excellence & Brotherhood

Trinity Grammar School is one of Sydney's most respected Anglican independent schools for boys. Set in the inner-western suburb of Summer Hill, Trinity has been educating young men from Pre-Kindergarten through to Year 12 since 1913.

We believe that education is about far more than academic results. It is about shaping young men who are resilient, compassionate, and equipped to lead lives of meaning. Our Christian ethos runs through every element of school life — from the classroom to the chapel, the sportsfield to the concert hall.

Trinity's distinctive culture — warm, aspirational, and deeply relational — helps every boy find his place, discover his strengths, and grow into the man he is meant to be.

Education in the Round

Trinity's curriculum spans every dimension of a boy's development — intellectual, physical, spiritual, and creative.

Academic Excellence

A challenging, evidence-based curriculum from Pre-K to Year 12 that inspires intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong love of learning. Our HSC results consistently place Trinity among NSW's leading schools.

Pastoral Care

Every boy belongs. Our structured pastoral care programme — anchored by dedicated form teachers, House communities, and school chaplains — ensures every student feels seen, supported, and valued throughout his time at Trinity.

Creative Arts
